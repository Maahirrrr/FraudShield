💰 Cost Analysis — Why PR-AUC Beats ROC-AUC for Fraud

In a dataset where only 0.17% of transactions are fraud, ROC-AUC is misleadingly optimistic (even Autoencoder scores 0.96). PR-AUC penalises false positives harshly — aligning directly with merchant chargeback costs. Our XGBoost ensemble saves an estimated $18,525 over baseline Logistic Regression on 284K transactions alone. Deployed on a D2C seller platform processing 50K orders/month, this translates to >₹5L/month fraud prevention at <0.1% false-positive rate.
