Data science & deep learning evaluation

Honest empirical metrics across 284,807 Kaggle ULB transactions (0.173% fraud). Zero leakage, time-ordered validation, and dollar cost trade-offs.

Why accuracy fails here

Predicting zero fraud everywhere scores 99.83% accuracy while missing 100% of attacks. We rank models strictly by PR-AUC and business cost.

How we blocked data leakage

RobustScaler fits strictly on X_train; SMOTE runs strictly inside training folds; LSTM validates strictly on unseen future Time hours.

Honest XGBoost vs DL finding

XGBoost leads tabular PR-AUC (0.8596), while PyTorch FraudLSTM beats XGBoost on future-hour sequences (0.8088 vs 0.7933 PR-AUC).
